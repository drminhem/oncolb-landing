WhatsApp inside OncoLB

Your professional number,
connected to your clinic.

Doctors and clinics can authorize OncoLB to connect their own WhatsApp Business account. The integration brings patient communication into the clinic's workflow.

  1. Connect through Meta. Sign in to OncoLB, open My WhatsApp and choose Connect my number. Complete Meta's authorization to select the business account and number you want to connect.
  2. Read and reply. Receive messages and attachments in the connected inbox, reply during WhatsApp's permitted reply window, and send approved appointment templates when configured.
  3. Choose what belongs in the chart. Review a message or document and select the patient before filing it. Connecting an inbox does not automatically file its conversations into patient charts.
  4. Control team access. The inbox owner chooses which named colleagues can read and reply. The owner can disconnect the number from OncoLB.

Onboarding additional practices is subject to Meta approval and account eligibility. Keeping the same number active in the phone app requires an eligible WhatsApp Business app account and Meta's coexistence setup. WhatsApp messaging rules and applicable charges continue to apply.
