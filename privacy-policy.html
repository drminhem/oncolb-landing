Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

OncoLB ("we", "us", or "our") is provided by Dr. Mohamad Minhem in Beirut, Lebanon. We process personal data for website communication and clinic messaging workflows, including WhatsApp-based communication. Clinics LB is the Meta application used to connect a clinic's or doctor's authorized WhatsApp Business account to OncoLB.

1. Data We Process

  • Contact data such as phone number, WhatsApp ID, and email.
  • Connected WhatsApp Business account and phone-number identifiers, account details, and authorization credentials used to operate the connection.
  • Message content and media sent to clinic communication channels.
  • Message metadata such as delivery status, timestamps, and technical logs.

2. Purpose of Processing

  • To operate clinic communication and patient support workflows.
  • To connect authorized WhatsApp Business accounts, receive and reply to messages, and send approved appointment reminders.
  • To file a message or attachment in a patient chart when an authorized clinic user explicitly chooses to do so.
  • To extract document text, transcribe audio, and support clinical documentation when the corresponding AI features are used.
  • To maintain service reliability, security monitoring, and auditability.
  • To respond to support requests and operational inquiries.

3. Sharing and Processors

We use service providers to operate the service. Meta provides WhatsApp messaging; Vercel hosts the application; and Supabase provides the database and document storage. Our backup workflow uses GitHub Actions to prepare backups and Backblaze B2 to store encrypted backup archives.

When email features are used, Resend processes the email content and attachments. When document extraction, transcription, or other AI features are used, the selected content is processed by the configured AI provider, which may be OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google. These features can involve content received through WhatsApp and subsequently used in the patient chart.

Providers process data under their applicable service terms. Depending on the provider and service configuration, processing may take place outside Lebanon.

4. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as needed for service operation, legal requirements, and legitimate business purposes.

Disconnecting a WhatsApp number stops its connection to OncoLB and clears its connection credentials. Disconnection does not itself delete existing inbox history or records already filed in a patient chart. To request deletion, follow our data deletion instructions.

5. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by contacting m.minhem1@gmail.com.

6. Contact

For privacy-related requests, contact: m.minhem1@gmail.com.
